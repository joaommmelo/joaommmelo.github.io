02Aptoide Connect

Aptoide Connect is a global B2B platform connecting app developers with alternative app distribution channels. It gives developers the tools to manage and distribute their apps, and gives distribution partners the infrastructure and integrations they need to run their app catalogues.

Platform & product

I own the product strategy and roadmap for Connect. It's a two-sided platform, so most product decisions mean balancing what developers need with what distribution partners need, alongside engineering and business priorities.

I regularly review usage data, service metrics and end-to-end workflows across Connect to identify friction, prioritise improvements and uncover new business opportunities.

Developers

Publish and manage Android and iOS apps, versions and in-app products, configure prices, run marketing campaigns, and track app performance, earnings and payouts across partner stores and countries.

Distribution partners

Access the Connect app catalogue, integrate app distribution into their stores, manage their app offerings, run promotions and monitor distribution performance and financial results.

Aptoide Connect (formerly Catappult) started as a side project and has grown into a global app distribution platform. It began with Android distribution; I led its expansion to iOS, which now supports alternative marketplaces in the EU, Brazil and Japan.

Technical direction & architecture

I drive the technical direction of Aptoide Connect, shaping technology stack decisions and service architecture. I work closely with engineering teams on service monitoring, performance, reliability and continuous improvement.

Pricing

I lead the product direction of Aptoide's pricing service, which supports B2B and B2C use cases. Integrated into Aptoide Connect, it enables developers to configure and manage app prices, while powering the pricing information presented to end users throughout their purchase journeys.

AI-first strategy & automation

Aptoide takes an AI-first approach to internal operations and product development. My work spans automating internal workflows with AI and developing tools that help developers interact with and manage Aptoide Connect.

03How I Work

  1. Understand the problem before building

    Before deciding what to build, I try to understand who has the problem and why. Talking directly to developers and partners often changes the question, not just the answer.

  2. Move fast without compromising technical foundations

    Small, quick iterations are the best way to learn. But on a platform developers rely on, shortcuts in data models and APIs tend to come back later, so I'm deliberate about where speed is worth it.

  3. Connect technical decisions to product outcomes

    My engineering background helps me follow the technical trade-offs. I try to explain them in product terms: what a decision means for developers, partners and the business.
